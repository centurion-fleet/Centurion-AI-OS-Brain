Taking one

  1. Plug the backup drive into this machine.
  2. Choose the drive in the Brain app, and press Back up now.
  3. Wait for Verified — not "complete". A backup nobody checked is not a backup.

From a terminal, the same thing: centurion backup --encrypt --output /media/<drive>/practice.cbk

The passphrase

The backup is encrypted with a passphrase you set and keep. Centurion does not hold a copy and cannot recover the archive without it. Write it down somewhere a business can find it — the safe, or your password manager — and keep a printed copy. Losing it means the backup is unreadable, which is the point: nobody else can read it either.

Weekly is the default because a working practice generates a week's worth of letters, returns and client correspondence. A monthly backup loses too much to be worth taking.
